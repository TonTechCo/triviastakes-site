Support

Need help with Trivia Stakes? We're happy to help.

Contact us

Email info@tontech.co and we'll get back to you. When reporting a problem, it helps to include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Common topics

  • Subscriptions & purchases. Subscriptions and item packs are billed through your Apple ID. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device. Restore purchases from within the app's store screen.
  • Community Topics. Topics created by players are screened before others can see them. To report a topic, use the Report action on it, or email us. See our Community Content Policy.
  • Your data. To request deletion of your account and data, use the in-app account-deletion option or email us. See our Privacy Policy.

Response time

We aim to respond within a couple of business days, and to act on reports of objectionable Community content within 24 hours.
